Research & Projects

Raj Shekhor Roy

Antigen Discovery Pipeline (Texas A&M, SPHERICAL)

  • Led development and optimization of a computational pipeline for antigen identification and characterization using bioinformatics and deep learning; the prototype showed promising performance detecting SARS-CoV-2 conserved epitopes [1].
  • Integrated structural analysis, sequence analysis, and machine learning to improve antigen prediction, validating predictions with experimental data through interdisciplinary teams [1].

Agentic BGC Pathway Analysis

  • Performed genome-based BGC annotation and gene analysis to identify biosynthetic proteins [1].
  • Developed a pathway inference pipeline connecting clusters to predicted metabolic pathways and secondary metabolites [1].

Protein Structure Reconstruction (University of Missouri, BML Lab)

  • DRCon — a dilated ResNet using distributed deep learning for interchain contact prediction of homodimers; surpassed the state-of-the-art at release [1].
  • ConComplex — a simulated-annealing tool to reconstruct protein structure [1].
  • DeepComplex — co-developed web server for protein structure prediction [1].
  • DRLcomplex — co-developed reinforcement-learning approach to reconstruct protein dimeric structures [1].
  • GDFold — contributed to a gradient-descent structure reconstruction method [1].

Protein Quality Assessment

  • MULTICOM_qa — hybrid method to estimate global fold quality; average top-1 TM-score for CASP15 assembly targets was 5.3% higher than standard AlphaFold-Multimer, and it ranked top in estimating global fold accuracy of protein assemblies in CASP15 [1].

Cryo-EM

  • Proficient with CryoSPARC and EMAN2; experienced in reconstructing electron density maps from 2D micrographs and refining them, with qualitative assessment for data integrity [1].
