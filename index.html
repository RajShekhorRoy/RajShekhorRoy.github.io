About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Texas A&M University working on protein structure, interaction and quality prediction, novel protein discovery, protein and antigen design, and cryo-EM [1]. I currently develop computational pipelines for antigen discovery and agentic biosynthetic gene cluster (BGC) pathway analysis, combining bioinformatics and deep learning [1].

Currently

  • Leading development of a computational pipeline for antigen identification and characterization; the prototype detected SARS-CoV-2 conserved epitopes (publication in progress) [1].
  • Building an agentic BGC pathway analysis pipeline connecting gene clusters to predicted metabolic pathways and secondary metabolites [1].
  • Mentoring PhD and undergraduate students at Texas A&M University and the University of Missouri [1].
