About

I am a computational researcher focused on protein structure, interaction and quality prediction, novel protein discovery, protein and antigen design, and cryo-EM. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Missouri-Columbia and currently work as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Texas A&M University, building deep learning and bioinformatics pipelines for antigen discovery and biosynthetic gene cluster analysis.
