About

AI for protein science and biomolecular design, including protein structure and property prediction, protein–protein and protein–peptide interaction modeling, sequence–structure–function modeling, and generative design of proteins and antigens.

Currently

    Developed CARTnet, a novel contact-aware deep learning framework for de novo and template-guided B-cell epitope prediction, achieving an AUPRC of 0.267 on the external test set versus 0.215 for BepiPred-3.
  • Leading development of a computational pipeline for antigen identification and characterization; the prototype detected SARS-CoV-2 conserved epitopes .
  • Building an agentic BGC pathway analysis pipeline connecting gene clusters to predicted metabolic pathways and secondary metabolites .
  • Mentoring PhD and undergraduate students at Texas A&M University and the University of Missouri .
